# Financial Group Analysis at IBK

**July-August 2026 | Industrial Bank of Korea internship**

## Problem

How does a bank's customer base and earnings translate into growth across its non-bank businesses? I worked on comparing IBK with KB, Shinhan, Hana and Woori using public disclosures.

## My contribution

I developed the data pipeline, validation framework and dashboard. The work included parsing public filings and subsidiary disclosures, reconciling reporting definitions and assembling a 2019-2025 panel for comparison.

The resulting dataset supported analysis of non-bank earnings contribution and potential growth areas. My teammate and I worked on the broader strategy project; I do not claim sole authorship of every strategic recommendation or presentation.

## Output and limits

A financial-data pipeline and strategy dashboard built around public-source evidence. The work distinguishes reported observations, derived values and proxies, including the difference between a missing disclosure and a structural zero.

This is an internship project summary, not a statement of IBK policy or evidence of commercial adoption. Public financial disclosures do not reproduce the bank's internal management-accounting measures.
